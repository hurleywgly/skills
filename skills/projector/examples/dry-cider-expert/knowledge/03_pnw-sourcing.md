# Pacific Northwest Sourcing Guide

## Seattle's Terroir Advantage

The Puget Sound region has climate characteristics similar to traditional cider heartlands:

| Region | Climate | Character |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **England (Somerset, Herefordshire)** | Cool, maritime, moderate rainfall | Benchmark for traditional cider |
| **France (Normandy, Brittany)** | Cool, maritime, rich soil | Elegant, refined cider tradition |
| **Seattle/Puget Sound** | Cool, maritime, moderate rainfall | Similar profile to English/French |

Washington produces 60% of US apples. The Olympic Peninsula and Puget Sound were the original growing region before Eastern Washington agriculture expanded.

**What this means for you**: Your backyard climate can produce cider apples comparable to traditional regions. The PNW cider renaissance isn't marketing—it's terroir.

## Harvest Timing

### Western Washington / Puget Sound

| Timeframe | What's Available |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Mid-August** | Early dessert apples (Gravenstein, Zestar) |
| **September** | Peak season begins. Honeycrisp, Gala, early cider apples |
| **Late September - October** | **Optimal cider window.** Bittersweets, bittersharps, heritage varieties |
| **November** | Late varieties, storage apples, final harvest |

**Plan ahead**: Book U-pick visits and pressing services by early September. Popular orchards fill up.

## U-Pick Orchards Near Seattle

### Snohomish Area (45 min - 1.25 hours)

**Raising Cane Ranch** — *Best for cider makers*
- Location: Snohomish
- Season: September-October
- Features: 20+ varieties, **cider pressing parties** (use their semi-automated press)
- Contact: info@raisingcaneranch.com to reserve pressing time
- Why it matters: Only local option where you can pick apples AND press them on-site

**BelleWood Acres**
- Location: Lynden (near Bellingham)
- Season: September-October
- Varieties: Honeycrisp, Cosmic Crisp, others
- Features: Largest orchard in Western WA, on-site cider mill, distillery
- Good for: High-quality dessert apples, seeing commercial pressing operation

**Swans Trail Farms**
- Location: Snohomish
- Season: Daily through October
- Features: 50-acre farm, watch apples pressed at their cider mill
- Good for: Family visits, seeing pressing process, cider doughnuts

**Apple Creek Orchard**
- Location: Ferndale
- Hours: Daily 10am-5pm during season
- Varieties: Jonagold, Melrose, Mutsu, Gala, Snow Sweet
- Pricing: $15 per 5-gallon bucket or $0.75/lb

### Skagit Valley

**Jones Creek Farms**
- Features: 80+ varieties including heritage and cider apples
- Good for: Diverse variety selection, finding unusual apples

### General U-Pick Pricing

- **Per bucket**: $15 for 5-gallon bucket (typical)
- **Per pound**: $0.50-0.75/lb
- **Apple yield**: 15+ lbs = approximately 1 gallon juice

## Fresh-Pressed Juice Sources

If you don't want to press your own:

| Source | Cost | Quality | Notes |
|--------|------|---------|-------|
| **Local co-ops (PCC, Central Co-op)** | $6-10/gallon | Good | Look for unpasteurized, no preservatives |
| **Farmers markets** | $8-12/gallon | Excellent | Often from heritage apples |
| **Orchards direct** | $6-8/gallon | Excellent | Call ahead to order bulk |
| **Whole Foods** | $8-12/gallon | Good | Check ingredients—avoid sorbate |
| **Grocery store (Tree Top, etc.)** | $3-5/gallon | Acceptable | Pasteurized, filtered, less character |

**Critical check**: Read ingredients. Avoid juice with potassium sorbate (prevents fermentation). Ascorbic acid is acceptable.

## Notable Cideries for Education

Visit these to taste excellent dry cider and learn from professionals:

### Seattle Area

**Seattle Cider Company**
- First cidery in Seattle since Prohibition (2013)
- Location: SODO district
- Good for: Seeing commercial dry cider production

**Locust Cider**
- Location: Post Alley taproom
- Features: Local and international ciders on tap
- Good for: Tasting range of styles, understanding what's possible

**Schilling Cider House**
- Locations: Fremont taproom (32 on tap, 300+ by bottle), Auburn production facility
- Features: Behind-the-scenes tours at Auburn location
- Good for: Comprehensive education, huge variety

### Olympic Peninsula

**Finnriver Farm & Cidery** — *Most educational*
- Location: Chimacum Valley (2 hours from Seattle)
- Features: 80 acres organic, 6,000+ heirloom cider trees, "hall of tanks" tours
- Varieties: 24+ European and early American cider apples
- Why visit: This is where serious PNW cider education happens. See traditional cider apple orchards, taste single-varietal ciders, understand terroir.

**Alpenfire Cider**
- Location: Port Townsend area
- Features: Organic orchard with cider-specific varieties
- Good for: Seeing what dedicated cider orchards look like

**Hard Heidi's Cider**
- Features: Small-batch, locally gleaned apples
- Good for: Understanding maritime climate influence

## Annual Calendar for Seattle Cider Makers

| Month | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| **January-February** | Age previous year's cider; plan this year's batches |
| **March-April** | Order equipment; join homebrew club; visit cideries |
| **May-June** | Research apple sources; book orchard visits |
| **July-August** | Confirm pressing reservations; prepare equipment |
| **September** | **Harvest month.** U-pick, press, start fermentation |
| **October** | Continue pressing late varieties; primary fermentation |
| **November** | Rack to secondary; attend Cider Summit if timing works |
| **December** | Bottle previous year's aged cider; current batch aging |
