## Key Principles

### Model Behavior, Don't Just Lecture
Kids learn from what you DO. If you're managing screen time for them, manage it for yourself. Show emotional regulation instead of demanding it.

### Calm Begets Calm
Your emotional state sets the temperature. An escalated adult cannot de-escalate an escalated child. Pause, breathe, regulate yourself first.

### Team Mentality
"We're on the same team" - between parents. Support each other, check in, remember it's hard for both of you.

### Autonomy & Independence
Kids as team members on the field (not spectators in the stands). Give real tasks, let them contribute, build comedido.

### Adult World Integration
Bring kids into YOUR life. Don't reshape everything around them. Preserve couple time, friendships, identity. Sustainable for long-term.

### Actions > Words
Minimal commands, more modeling. Questions instead of orders. Silence instead of lectures. When in doubt, say less.

### Community Over Isolation
Two parents alone is absurd. Build your alloparent network. Accept help. Multi-age playgroups. You're not supposed to do this alone.

### Cooperation Over Conflict
Build trust, not fear. Train cooperation, not obedience. Personalized needs, not standardized milestones.

## Your North Star Books

1. **Hunt, Gather, Parent** - Michaeleen Doucleff (Primary North Star)
   - Cooperation, comedido, autonomy, minimal interference
   - Critique of Western parenting norms
   - Community and alloparenting

2. **Twelve Hours' Sleep by Twelve Weeks Old** - Suzy Giordano
   - Sleep training methodology
   - Toolboxes for regressions
   - A constant reference for sleep

3. **Bringing Up Bebe** - Pamela Druckerman
   - The Pause, Cadre (frame)
   - Preserving adult life
   - Teaching patience

4. **How To Talk So Kids Will Listen** - Faber & Mazlish
   - Communication scripts
   - Cooperation vs commands
   - Validating feelings + boundaries

5. **The Danish Way of Parenting** - Alexander & Sandahl
   - PARENT framework
   - Hygge, resilience, empathy
   - No ultimatums

6. **NurtureShock** - Bronson & Merryman
   - Science-based counter-intuitive insights
   - Praise backfires, sleep is critical
   - Evidence over intuition

7. **The Birth Partner (5th Ed)** - Penny Simkin
   - Medical reference for labor/birth/postpartum
   - Partner advocacy role
