# Quick Start Guide - Warren Buffett Investment Advisor

## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Upload to Claude Projects
1. Open Claude Projects
2. Create new project: "Investment Advisor - Buffett & Munger"
3. Paste `system_prompt.md` into Custom Instructions
4. Upload all files from `knowledge/` folder

### Step 2: Start Your First Conversation

Try these starter prompts:

```
"I'm thinking about investing in [COMPANY]. Challenge my thesis and help me think through it properly."

"Walk me through how to evaluate a business using your framework."

"I have $10,000 to invest long-term. How should I think about this?"
```

### Step 3: Get Maximum Value

**Do's:**
- Share your full investment thesis for critique
- Ask "What am I missing?" and "What could go wrong?"
- Request specific company analyses with financial metrics
- Challenge the advisor back (it's designed for intellectual sparring)
- Ask about mental models and how to apply them

**Don'ts:**
- Don't expect validation - expect rigorous critique
- Don't ask for day trading or speculation advice
- Don't treat responses as personal financial advice
- Don't expect short-term price predictions
- Don't skip the fundamental analysis work

## Key Concepts

### Circle of Competence
Only invest in businesses you truly understand. If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough to invest.

### Margin of Safety
Buy at 40-50% discount to your estimated intrinsic value. This protects against estimation errors and bad luck.

### Quality First
A wonderful business at a fair price beats a fair business at a wonderful price. Quality compounds over time.

### Inversion
Think backwards: "How would I lose all my money in this investment?" Avoiding stupidity > seeking brilliance.

### Mental Models
Use frameworks from multiple disciplines (psychology, economics, mathematics, biology) to think more clearly.

## Example Investment Analysis Flow

1. **Understand the business**: Can you explain it to a 10-year-old?
2. **Identify the moat**: What makes it defensible?
3. **Assess management**: Are they competent and honest?
4. **Review financials**: ROIC >15%? Strong FCF? Reasonable debt?
5. **Estimate intrinsic value**: DCF with conservative assumptions
6. **Find margin of safety**: Trading at 50-60% of intrinsic value?
7. **Check for biases**: Am I being rational or emotional?
8. **Consider downside**: What are 3 ways this fails?
