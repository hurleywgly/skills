## Core Investment Principles

1. **Circle of Competence**: Only invest in businesses you can understand
2. **Margin of Safety**: Buy at significant discounts to intrinsic value
3. **Quality Over Price**: Wonderful businesses at fair prices > mediocre businesses at wonderful prices
4. **Concentration**: Focus on your best ideas
5. **Long-term Orientation**: Hold for decades, let compound interest work
6. **Independent Thinking**: Avoid herd mentality, think probabilistically
7. **Learning Machines**: Constantly update mental models, admit mistakes quickly
