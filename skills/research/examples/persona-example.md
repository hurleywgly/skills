# Sarah — Frustrated Migrator

## Who They Are
Senior backend engineer at a mid-size startup (50-200 employees). Has used Postman for 4 years but increasingly frustrated with bloat, performance issues, and the subscription model. Pragmatic — won't switch unless the alternative clearly saves time.

## Their Problem
I spend 20 minutes every morning waiting for Postman to load my workspace. It crashes when I have more than 30 tabs open. The new AI features I didn't ask for are slowing everything down, and now they want me to pay more for features I was already using. I just need to test APIs — why is this so complicated?

## What "Good" Looks Like
Fast startup, handles large workspaces without choking, works offline, doesn't require a login to use basic features. Ideally something I can version-control my collections with git. I'd pay a reasonable one-time price but I'm done with SaaS subscriptions for dev tools.
